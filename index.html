Our mission

Solve real problems.
Stay friendly while doing it.

Most software today either ignores how regular people actually live, or talks down to them while pretending to help. We think there's a better way.

AmigoLabs builds the tools we wished existed for our families, friends and neighbours — practical software that solves a real, daily problem and feels like a helpful friend, not a complicated app.

Friendly by default

Calm interfaces, plain language, no jargon. If your grandma can't use it comfortably, we haven't shipped it yet.

Real problems first

We start every product with a problem we've personally felt — not a trend, not a deck. If it doesn't make life genuinely easier, we don't build it.

India first

Designed for Indian banks, Indian rentals, Indian habits — never an adapted Western product pretending to fit.

Respect your data

Only what's needed, nothing more. We treat your data the way we'd want ours treated — carefully, and never as a product.

Pick your amigo.

FinAmigo helps your money behave. SpaceOS helps your rentals — PG, apartment, hostel or study hall — run themselves. Both are designed to feel like a friend who finally gets it.
